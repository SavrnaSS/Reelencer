Network failures retry from two minutes, login walls and rate limits from thirty minutes, and credential/missing-account errors from one hour. Consecutive failures increase the delay up to six hours with stable account jitter; platform `Retry-After` values are respected up to twenty-four hours. A failed attempt updates `last_sync_attempt_at`, never `last_synced_at` or saved measurements. Both dashboards display the last successful update, next check/retry, connection source, and actual progress rather than presenting a failed attempt as fresh data. Manual requests have a one-minute cooldown and cannot steal leases, sync paused accounts or bypass a platform rate-limit deadline. New connection settings enqueue waiting accounts for recovery.
